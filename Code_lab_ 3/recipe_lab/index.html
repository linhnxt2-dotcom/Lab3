Grandma's Sunday Pancakes 🥞

Ingredients Steps Notes

About This Recipe

These fluffy pancakes have been a family favorite for three generations. Perfect for a lazy Sunday morning with a cup of coffee.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 3/4 cups milk
  • 1/4 cup melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Mix all dry ingredients in a large bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk eggs, milk, butter, and vanilla.
  3. Combine wet and dry ingredients until just mixed.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled griddle over medium heat.
  5. Pour batter and cook until bubbles form, then flip.
  6. Serve warm with your favorite toppings, such as:
    • Maple syrup
    • Fresh berries
    • Whipped cream

Cooking Terms

Whisk
To beat ingredients rapidly to mix or incorporate air.
Griddle
A flat cooking surface used for pancakes, eggs, and similar foods.
Fold
To gently combine ingredients without deflating air bubbles.
